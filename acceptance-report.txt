Bradley-Terry Boundary Behavior
------------------------------
The project distinguishes genuine convergence from iteration-cap termination.
For well-connected, non-boundary examples, the engine converges normally.
For degenerate or boundary MLE cases, the skill vector can collapse to the edge of the
parameter space and the solver may terminate at max_iterations with converged = false.
This is consistent with the algorithm's limiting behavior and is not a forced
convergence artifact. The suite therefore treats "converged = false" as an expected
iteration-cap outcome only for boundary cases, while well-connected examples must
converge.
